• "support services". This restriction applies universally, regardless of whether the system is managed by a traditional on-site warden, a housing association, or an external private technology company.

Sub-paragraph (g): Services Not Connected with Adequate Accommodation

Because automated daily contact does not fix physical wear-and-tear, clean communal areas, or maintain structural safety, it fails the "adequate accommodation" test. It is classified as an operational cost tied to individual tenant well-being, rather than a landlord maintenance expense.
